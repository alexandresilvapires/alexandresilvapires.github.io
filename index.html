About

Hey! I'm Alex, a Ph.D. student at the Prosocial Dynamics Lab, part of the Socially Intelligent Artificial Systems (SIAS) Group, University of Amsterdam. My work focuses on understanding how human indirect reciprocity (reputation-based cooperation) can be affected by artificial agents (chatbots, robots, etc.). I'm supervised by Fernando P. Santos (SIAS) and Ana Paiva (GAIPS). My formal education is on artificial intelligence and complex systems. However, I always strive to bring perspectives from various fields, such as music, art, history, economics, ecology, and more. Before my Ph.D., I did my Computer Science and Engineering master's degree at Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon, Portugal, supervised by Francisco C. Santos and Jorge M. Pacheco.
